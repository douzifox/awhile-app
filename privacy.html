Awhile 隐私政策

更新于 2026-08-28

Awhile 没有自己的服务器，不收集任何统计或遥测数据，不接入任何第三方 SDK。你的数据只存在两个地方：这台设备，和你自己配置的服务。

数据存储

聊天记录、记忆、朋友圈、日志和文件全部保存在本机数据库中，API Key 加密存储。删除好友或重置聊天时，相关数据在本机删除。卸载 App 会删除全部数据。

AI 服务商

发消息时，聊天内容以及注入的当前时间、天气、城市名、记忆和朋友圈摘要，会由设备直接发送给你自己配置的 AI 服务商用于生成回复。发送范围以各服务商的隐私政策为准，Awhile 不经手任何中转，开发者无法看到这些内容。

定位与天气

仅在全局设置开启「定位与天气」后才会获取位置。位置用于向 Apple 天气获取本地天气和城市名，坐标只存本机、不会发给 AI 服务商——进入聊天的只有城市名和天气文本。关闭开关后位置与天气缓存立即删除。

健康数据

仅在全局设置开启「健康数据」后，AI 才能查询你的 Apple 健康数据，样本保存在本机，只读不写。关闭开关后本地健康数据立即删除。健康数据不会用于任何用途以外的目的，也不会提供给除你配置的 AI 服务商以外的任何第三方。

麦克风与语音识别

麦克风仅在你录制语音消息或语音通话时使用；语音输入使用系统语音识别。

通知

所有通知都是本地通知，由设备自己生成，没有推送服务器。

iCloud 备份

开启自动备份后，备份包存入你自己的 iCloud 账户容器，滚动保留最近几份，开发者无法访问。

MCP 服务

如果你在设置中自行添加了第三方 MCP 工具服务，AI 调用工具时的内容会发送给该服务。是否添加、添加哪些，完全由你决定，App 不预置任何端点。

儿童

Awhile 面向 17 岁以上用户，不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。

变更

本政策如有变更会更新本页面及顶部日期。

Awhile Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-08-28

Awhile has no server of its own, collects no analytics or telemetry, and includes no third-party SDKs. Your data lives in only two places: this device, and the services you configure yourself.

Data Storage

Chats, memories, moments, journals and files are all stored in the on-device database; API keys are stored encrypted. Deleting a friend or resetting a chat removes the data on this device. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

AI Providers

When you send a message, the conversation — along with the injected time, weather, city name, memories and moments summary — is sent directly from the device to the AI provider you configured, to generate the reply. Their privacy policies apply. Awhile relays nothing through other servers, and the developer cannot see any of it.

Location & Weather

Location is only accessed after you enable Location & Weather in Settings. It is used to fetch local weather and your city name from Apple Weather. Coordinates stay on this device and are never sent to AI providers — only the city name and weather text enter the chat. Turning the switch off deletes the cached location and weather immediately.

Health Data

Only after you enable Health Data in Settings can the AI query your Apple Health data. Samples are stored on this device, read-only. Turning the switch off deletes the local health data immediately. Health data is never used for any other purpose and never shared with anyone other than the AI provider you configured.

Microphone & Speech

The microphone is used only while you record voice messages or during voice calls; voice input uses the system speech recognizer.

Notifications

All notifications are local, generated on the device. There is no push server.

iCloud Backup

With auto backup on, backups are saved to your own iCloud account container with a few rolling copies kept. The developer has no access to them.

MCP Servers

If you add third-party MCP tool servers in Settings, tool-call content is sent to those servers. Whether and which to add is entirely your choice; the app ships with no preconfigured endpoints.

Children

Awhile is intended for users 17 and older and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with the date above updated.
